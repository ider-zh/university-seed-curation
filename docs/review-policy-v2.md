# Source-grounded flexible review, private pilot v2

Operator steering during the ten-case pilot explicitly permits reasonable
concept adaptation. This policy changes acceptance criteria; all earlier strict
verdicts remain archived. It does not authorize changing a source fact.

- Campus officially opened/put into use in year Y may populate its valid_from.
  It means the start of the represented operational phase, not a proved earliest
  physical existence. Do not reject it merely because construction or informal
  activity may predate it. Source wording and locator must remain available.
- A leader inauguration/installation year may populate term_start as a reasonable inference, without proof that legal appointment, election and ceremony all occurred on the same date. Keep year precision if only the year is given.
- A reasonably broader controlled field or event may represent a source concept
  when its subject and source meaning remain recoverable from original wording,
  description, evidence or attached case qualifications. Do not demand exact
  wording equivalence or an unavailable specialized enum.
- Preserve the source's date precision, numerical value/unit, original title and
  statistical scope. Source qualifications retained in cited wording can be
  acceptable for this inspection pilot even when a dedicated normalized scope
  field is absent; disclose that downstream ranking needs explicit scope parsing.
- Do not reject an accurate fact because its source has an unknown year, a single
  source family, no officeholder end date or an approximate value.
- This is bounded core-field extraction, not an exhaustive article transcription.
  Missing optional milestones, membership events, awards and every last article
  fact are coverage limitations, not automatic reasons to reject supported data.
- Wrong institution/campus/faculty, wrong numerical value, lost currency or FTE
  meaning, fabricated dates, unsupported legal independence and source-incompatible
  interpretation remain errors. A current-country coordinate must not become an
  historical jurisdiction or a city coordinate become an exact campus coordinate.
- Distinguish direct support from reasonable inference in each review check. An inference must be anchored to cited source facts and explained; do not turn an invented value into an inference.
- Approve source-supported representation with clear qualifications. Keep real
  conflicts visible. Do not force a single definitive historical narrative.
